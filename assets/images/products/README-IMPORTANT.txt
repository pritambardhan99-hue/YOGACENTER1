UPLOAD YOUR PRODUCT PHOTOS HERE
================================
1. Upload each product photo into this folder (assets/images/products/).
   ANY format works (JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, AVIF) and ANY size/resolution
   — the product card automatically crops/fits every photo into a clean
   square frame, so it always looks neat regardless of the original photo's
   shape or size.
2. Open products.php and, for each product in the $products array near the
   top, set "img" to your uploaded filename (e.g. "premium-protein.jpg").
3. Leave "img" as "" (empty) for any product and a neat placeholder icon box
   is shown automatically until you add the real photo.

Recommended (not required): photos roughly square and under ~2MB load the
fastest, but the layout will look correct with any size you upload.
